Night mode

Slide fully to the left for Off: the display stays dark at night, and a press of the button lights it for a minute. If “From” and “Until” are the same, night mode is off.
Enter the callsign, flight number, aircraft registration or ICAO address, for example BAW123, BA123, G-EUUA or 4CA7B5. Shown on the display in large letters before the flight's card, until tracking stops. Up to 20 letters, digits and simple punctuation; accented letters are shown without accents.

While a flight is tracked, show

The display switches to the tracked flight right away and shows the chosen windows in turn. It stays there until you change the view or press Stop. After landing it shows LANDED for 2 more minutes, then tracking stops and the previous view comes back. The card or the map always stays on.

Flight data

For example http://192.168.0.10:8080. The server gives each display its own access key automatically. Optional. Adds flights that the AeroBox data does not see. Create a free account at opensky-network.org, then make an API client on your Account page.
Every update is one request on your plan; a tracked flight outside your range adds at most one request every 30 seconds.
